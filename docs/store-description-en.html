The walls between dimensions are breaking. Defend two worlds at once.

For reasons no one understands, interference between dimensions has grown violent. Monsters from neighboring dimensions have begun pouring across the border.

2CTRL is a top-down survival game where the screen is split in two and a single player controls both characters at the same time, holding back the monsters crossing into two dimensions.

Left hand on WASD, right hand on the arrow keys. Attacks are automatic. All you have to do is keep both characters alive. Sounds easy? Wait until your hands start moving in different directions.


What makes it different

▶ Monsters crossing over from the other dimension

Before each run, the two characters each take a different dimension: Forest, Factory or Storm. Your dimension sets your starting weapon, weapon pool and map. The monsters you fight, however, are the ones crossing over from the other dimension. Every pairing of dimensions plays differently.


▶ When one side grows stronger, so do its monsters

Both characters share experience, and on each level-up both sides pick their own reward. But the two dimensions are entangled. When one dimension gains power, the monsters crossing over from it grow stronger too, and the character on the other side is put at a disadvantage. Build up only one side and the other will fall.


▶ 30 minutes, 14 waves, and the Lord of Dimensions

Survive 14 escalating waves and mid-bosses at the 10 and 20-minute marks, and the final boss, the Lord of Dimensions, appears across both screens. Both characters chip away at one shared health bar. If either one falls, the run is over.


Key features

  • Two characters, one player - two screens, two hands, two battlefields at once
  • Dimensional interference - take two of three dimensions (Forest, Factory, Storm) and hold back the monsters crossing over from the other side
  • Entangled growth - powering up one side also strengthens the monsters it sends across
  • 18 weapons with 10 upgrade levels - special ★ effects unlock at levels 5 and 10
  • 6 dimension weapons - fused weapons that combine the power of both dimensions and strike across both screens
  • 7 bosses - two mid-bosses per dimension, plus a final boss that stretches across both screens
  • Permanent upgrades - earn Dimension Power after each run and raise stats shared by both characters
  • Monster keyword system - 10 combinable behaviors such as dash, teleport, split, self-destruct and revive
